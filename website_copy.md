## 2. Find Your Level (quiz)

**Section title:** Find Your Level

**Intro:** Answer 4 quick questions. Get your level, your moves, and your next goal.

### Questions

**Q1. How much do you have saved?**
- A. Under $1K
- B. $1K to $5K
- C. More than $5K, or 3 months of bills

**Q2. Do you have debt over 15% interest?**
- A. Yes
- B. No

**Q3. Are you investing yet?**
- A. Not yet
- B. Yes

**Q4. How much is invested?** (only show if Q3 = Yes; if Q3 = Not yet, treat as Under $100K)
- A. Under $100K
- B. $100K to $600K
- C. Near $600K, or 2 years from quitting
- D. $1M+

### How to pick the result (check top to bottom, first match wins)

| If | Result |
|---|---|
| Q4 = $1M+ | The Dynasty |
| Q4 = Near $600K, or 2 years from quitting | The Flip |
| Q4 = $100K to $600K | The Accelerator |
| Q1 = Under $1K, or Q2 = Yes | Ground Zero |
| Q1 = $1K to $5K | The Cushion |
| Q1 = More than $5K, or 3 months of bills | The Builder |

### Results

**Ground Zero**
- Where you are: $0 to $1K, paycheck to paycheck
- Your moves: Learn a skill that pays, add side income, sell what you don't use, cut non-essentials, kill 15%+ debt
- Goal: $1K cash. Stop living at $0.

**The Cushion**
- Where you are: $1K to $5K saved
- Your moves: Open a HYSA, auto transfer on payday, pay yourself first instead of saving what's left, grab the full 401(k) match
- Goal: $5K or 3 months of bills saved

**The Builder**
- Where you are: Cushion done
- Your moves: Open the 4 accounts, run Phase 1, invest 15 to 25% of take-home pay, keep lifestyle the same as income grows
- Goal: $100K invested

**The Accelerator**
- Where you are: $100K+ invested
- Your moves: Max the Roth every year, backdoor Roth if your income gets too high, half of every raise goes to investing, keep the hedges
- Goal: Freedom Ratio hits 50%

**The Flip**
- Where you are: Near $600K or 2 years from quitting
- Your moves: Rotate growth into income in long term gain chunks across 2 tax years, build 1 to 2 years of cash in a HYSA
- Goal: Freedom Ratio hits 100%

**The Dynasty**
- Where you are: $1M+
- Your moves: Protect it, pass it down
- Goal: Assets pay the bills forever

## 3. Freedom Ratio Calculator

**Section title:** Your Freedom Ratio

**Intro:** One number tells you how close you are to free. Monthly income from your investments divided by your monthly bills. At 100%, your assets pay your bills and work becomes optional.

**Inputs:**
- Monthly bills ($)
- Monthly investment income ($)

**Formula:** Freedom Ratio = Monthly investment income ÷ Monthly bills × 100

**Output:** "Your Freedom Ratio: [X]%" plus the matching meaning below.

| If your ratio is | Show |
|---|---|
| Under 10% | Just getting started. Every dollar counts. |
| 10% to 24% | Your money covers your phone and streaming |
| 25% to 49% | It covers your car and insurance |
| 50% to 99% | Half your life is paid for |
| 100% to 149% | Free. Work is optional. |
| 150%+ | Dynasty. Extra keeps compounding for the kids. |

If monthly bills is empty or $0, show: "Enter your monthly bills to see your ratio."

**Note under the calculator:** Know your monthly bills to the dollar. You can't hit a target you haven't measured.
